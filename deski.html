Deski.

A small desk robot that keeps you focused, with your own stake on the line. It's a Tamagotchi style wellness desk companion.

Physical AI is next. Deski sees you, decides on 0G whether you're focused, and settles the result on Hedera.

What it does

Six things Deski can do

Talk and chat

Hold the button, talk, let go. 0G turns your speech into text and writes the reply. Deski says it out loud and types it on the screen.

"What should I focus on today?"

Focus sessions

Ask for a session and the backend stakes ATTN on Hedera. If you don't give a length, the session lasts 2 minutes.

"Stake 2 ATTN for 25 minutes."

Camera checks

Every 10 seconds Deski takes a photo. 0G vision describes it, and a second 0G call decides if you're focused.

"User is looking at their phone."

Warn, then slash

The first miss gets a warning and a neck exercise. A second miss slashes the stake, and the session ends.

"We have slashed your stake. 1 ATTN invested"

Win it back

When time is up, Deski asks for three slow neck rotations and takes three photos. If 0G sees you do it, the stake comes back.

"Unstaked 1 ATTN. Focus session ended."

ENS names

Ask about any .eth name. The backend looks it up on the ENS subgraph and hands back the site link.

"Look up vitalik.eth" → vitalik.eth.limo
Hackathon partners

How it thinks, how it stakes, who it finds

Three partners, each covering one part of Deski. Items marked Live run in the current build.

Intelligence partner

How it thinks

Every model call goes to 0G, and there is no fallback. If 0G doesn't answer, Deski says so and decides nothing.

0G ComputeLive

Whisper for speech, the 0G chat model for replies, vision for camera photos, and the backend's tool decision. Each call runs in a TEE and is checked with verify_tee.

0G StorageLive

Every /tool decision is logged in the background with two 0G request ids: the device's call and the backend's decision.

Economy partner

How it stakes

The stake is an ATTN token on Hedera testnet, managed with Stablecoin Studio.

StakeLive

Mint ATTN to your account, then freeze it so it can't move during the session. Each stake mints 1 ATTN for now.

Unstake and slashLive

Both unfreeze and wipe the ATTN. Unstake ends the session cleanly, and slash is what happens after the second lost focus.

USDC in, USDC backNext

The USDC transfer is written but switched off until the test account has testnet USDC.

Identity partner

Who it finds

Deski can look up ENS names out loud, so a spoken name turns into a link you can open.

ENS subgraphLive

The backend checks the name on the ENS subgraph. If the lookup itself fails, it tells you to try again instead of saying the name doesn't exist.

eth.limo linkLive

A found name comes back as https://name.eth.limo.

Decision loop

How a focus session runs

Voice turns and camera checks both end up at the backend's /tool endpoint. There, one 0G call picks the action, Hedera settles it, and the decision is logged on 0G Storage.
